How Our Zero-Bloat Tailwind v4 Compiler Works

Why are browser defaults stripped automatically?

Copying computed styles from DevTools typically dumps hundreds of lines like position: static, opacity: 1, or flex properties on non-flex containers. Our engine filters non-semantic defaults and collapses 4-side padding/margin shorthands into concise utilities like py-4 px-6.

Does it support exact custom colors and pixel values?

Yes. Standard Tailwind palette tokens (such as bg-slate-900 or bg-blue-600) map directly to built-in classes, while bespoke brand hex values and non-4px dimensions cleanly emit Tailwind v4 arbitrary utilities like bg-[#1a1f36] and text-[15px].
